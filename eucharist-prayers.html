Pre-Communion Prayers
Words to carry with you on Sunday morning.
Invocation of the Spirit
O Heavenly King
Βασιλεῦ Οὐράνιε · Universal Orthodox prayer

O Heavenly King, the Comforter, the Spirit of Truth,
who art everywhere present and fillest all things,
Treasury of good things and Giver of life:
Come and abide in us,
and cleanse us from every impurity,
and save our souls,
O Good One.

Amen.
Confession of Faith
I Believe, O Lord, and I Confess
St. John Chrysostom · The central pre-Communion prayer

I believe, O Lord, and I confess that Thou art truly the Christ, the Son of the living God, who camest into the world to save sinners, of whom I am first.

I believe also that this is truly Thine own pure Body, and that this is truly Thine own precious Blood.

Therefore, I pray Thee: have mercy on me and forgive me my transgressions, both voluntary and involuntary, of word and of deed, of knowledge and of ignorance; and make me worthy to partake without condemnation of Thy most pure Mysteries, for the remission of my sins and unto life everlasting.

Amen.
Communion Hymn
Of Thy Mystical Supper
Sung at the Divine Liturgy of St. John Chrysostom

Of Thy Mystical Supper, O Son of God, accept me today as a communicant; for I will not speak of the Mystery to Thine enemies, neither like Judas will I give Thee a kiss; but like the thief will I confess Thee: Remember me, O Lord, in Thy Kingdom.

Amen.
Approach Prayer
O Master, Lord Jesus Christ
St. John of Damascus · Short form

O Master, Lord Jesus Christ, our God, who alone hast authority to forgive the sins of men: in Thy goodness and love, overlook all my offenses, whether committed with knowledge or in ignorance, and make me worthy to partake without condemnation of Thy divine, glorious, pure, and life-giving Mysteries; not for punishment, nor for greater condemnation, but for the purification and sanctification of soul and body, and as a pledge of the life and Kingdom to come.

Amen.
Glory to God for all things.
