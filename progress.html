About These Streaks

Weekly Streak counts the number of weeks in a row you completed your expedition session.

Prayer Streak counts how many days in a row you prayed both Morning and Evening prayer.

Each week, missing one prayer slot or one session day is gently absorbed — your streak holds. A small gold dot beside the title means grace was used this week. Glory to God for your faithfulness.
