## ✦ Sequence — The Order of Morning Prayer

Your morning prayer rule has a specific order. Number these from **1** to **6** in the boxes below.

  ☐  Cross yourself: *In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.*

  ☐  Ask God to bless the day ahead: *Lord, bless this day. Be with me in everything I do. Help me to love. Amen.*

  ☐  Stand before your icons. Light the candle.

  ☐  Pray the Lord's Prayer slowly: *Our Father, Who art in heaven…*

  ☐  Pray *Holy God, Holy Mighty, Holy Immortal, have mercy on us.* (Three times)

  ☐  Cross yourself one more time. You are ready.

## ✦ Reflection — Write 3 to 5 Sentences

The lesson said: *five minutes a day, every day. That is the rule.* Five minutes faithfully kept becomes a real spiritual life over decades. Write 3 to 5 sentences. What was it like to set up your prayer corner this week? What did it feel like to stand in front of your own icons in your own home? Did anything feel hard? *It's okay if your mind wandered. It's okay if it felt awkward. Most Orthodox Christians say it took weeks before standing at the corner started to feel natural. You are at the very beginning.*
