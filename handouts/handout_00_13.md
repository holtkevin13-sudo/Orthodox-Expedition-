This is from the story of Elias at Mount Horeb. ## ✦ Vocabulary — Match Each Word to Its Meaning

Draw a line from each word on the left to its meaning on the right.

  Prophet           •     •  The mountain in Israel where Elias defeated
                             the four hundred and fifty priests of Baal —
                             fire from heaven consumed his altar after he
                             prayed.

  Carmel            •     •  Greek for *spokesman* — a person sent by God
                             to speak His word to His people, especially
                             when they have forgotten Him.

  Horeb             •     •  The chariot of fire that took Elias up alive
                             into heaven. He is one of only two people in
                             the Bible who never died.

  Whirlwind         •     •  The mountain of Moses (also called Sinai),
                             where God came to Elias not in wind, fire,
                             or earthquake — but in a sound of fine
                             silence.

## ✦ Reflection — Write 3 to 5 Sentences

The lesson said: *the loudest moments of your life are not always where God meets you.* God came to Elias not in the wind, not in the earthquake, not in the fire — but in the silence after. Write 3 to 5 sentences. Can you think of a quiet moment in your life when something important happened — when you heard something, or noticed something, or knew something — that you would have missed if you had been listening for thunder? *It's okay if you can't think of one yet. It's okay to write that you're going to start listening.*
