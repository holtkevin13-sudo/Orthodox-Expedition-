## ✦ Vocabulary — Match Each Word to Its Meaning

Draw a line from each word on the left to its meaning on the right.

  Patron Saint       •     •  Greek for *Wonderworker* — a title given
                              to saints whose lives manifested
                              extraordinary miraculous gifts. St.
                              Herman is one.

  Wonderworker       •     •  Your particular friend in heaven, given
   (Thaumaturgos)              to you at your Chrismation. Knows your
                              name. Prays for you specifically. His
                              feast is part of your year now.

  Glorification      •     •  The original people of Kodiak Island and
                              the Aleutian chain in Alaska, whom St.
                              Herman defended against Russian fur
                              traders and called *abba* — father.

  Aleut people       •     •  The moment when the Orthodox Church
                              officially recognizes someone as a saint.
                              St. Herman was glorified in 1970.

## ✦ Reflection — Write 3 to 5 Sentences

St. Herman left his home, his country, and his language to go to the end of the world for the Aleut people no one else would defend. The lesson said: *that is the shape of his holiness — he loved the people no one else would love.* Write 3 to 5 sentences. What is something hard you'd be willing to do for someone you love? Or — are there people in your life, at school or in your neighborhood, who no one else seems to love or defend? What might it look like to follow your patron, in a small way? *It's okay to keep this short. Even one sentence about one specific person is enough.*
