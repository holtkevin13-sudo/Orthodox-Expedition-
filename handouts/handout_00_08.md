## ✦ Vocabulary — Match Each Word to Its Meaning

Draw a line from each word on the left to its meaning on the right.

  Divine Liturgy        •     •  The central double doors of the
                                 iconostasis. Father Nick passes through
                                 them at the Little Entrance, the Great
                                 Entrance, and Communion.

  Anaphora              •     •  Greek for *release* — the dismissal at
                                 the end of the Liturgy. You are sent
                                 out to carry Christ into your week.

  Royal Doors           •     •  The longest, most important prayer of
                                 the Liturgy. Father Nick calls down the
                                 Holy Spirit; the bread becomes the Body
                                 of Christ, the wine becomes the Blood.

  Apolysis              •     •  The Orthodox Sunday worship service —
                                 a journey that begins at the church
                                 door, moves toward the chalice, and
                                 sends you back out into the world.

## ✦ Layout — The Six Landmarks of the Liturgy

The Divine Liturgy has six landmarks. They unfold in the same order in every Orthodox church in the world. Use the word bank to fill in each blank.

**Word bank:** *Little Entrance · Gospel Reading · Great Entrance · Anaphora · Communion · Dismissal*

|  #  | Landmark             | What Happens                                       |
|-----|----------------------|----------------------------------------------------|
|  1  |  __________________  | Father Nick processes the Gospel book through the nave. |
|  2  |  __________________  | The Gospel is read aloud. Listen for *Wisdom! Be attentive!* |
|  3  |  __________________  | Father Nick processes the chalice and diskos to the altar. |
|  4  |  __________________  | The longest prayer. The Holy Spirit comes down on the gifts. The bread becomes the Body of Christ. |
|  5  |  __________________  | The choir sings *With fear of God, with faith and love, draw near.* You receive Christ. |
|  6  |  __________________  | Father Nick blesses the people. *Glory to Thee, O Christ our God.* You are sent. |
