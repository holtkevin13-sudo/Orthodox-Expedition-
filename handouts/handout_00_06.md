## ✦ Vocabulary — Match Each Word to Its Meaning

Draw a line from each word on the left to its meaning on the right.

  Sealed              •     •  Greek for *bread set aside* — the small
                               piece of blessed (but not consecrated)
                               bread you take after the chalice every
                               Sunday from now on.

  Indwelling          •     •  Marked permanently. The Holy Spirit is
                               not a guest who might leave; He is the
                               One who lives in you now, forever.

  New Creation        •     •  The Holy Spirit *living inside you* —
                               not visiting, but dwelling. Yesterday
                               evening, He came to make His home in you.

  Antidoron           •     •  Who you are now. The old you was buried
                               in the water. The new you came up out of
                               the water — washed clean, born again.

## ✦ Sequence — The Eight Anointings on Your Body

Yesterday evening, Father Nick anointed you with Holy Chrism in eight specific places. Each one was sealed with the words *The Seal of the Gift of the Holy Spirit. Amen.* Match each anointing to what it means.

  Forehead          •     •  *You will hear His voice.*

  Eyes              •     •  *Your heart is His now.*

  Nostrils          •     •  *Your mind belongs to Christ.*

  Lips              •     •  *You will breathe His Spirit.*

  Ears              •     •  *The path you walk is His.*

  Chest             •     •  *You will see the world with His eyes.*

  Hands             •     •  *You will speak His words.*

  Feet              •     •  *The work of your hands is His.*

## ✦ Reflection — Write 3 to 5 Sentences

Tomorrow morning is Sunday. You will walk into SJTD as a baptized, chrismated Orthodox Christian for the first time, and you will go to the chalice. Write 3 to 5 sentences about how you are feeling this morning, the day after the rite. Did it feel powerful? Quiet? Too much? Not what you expected? *Whatever it was, write it. Even if it's just a few words. Even if it's hard to put into words at all. The truth of yesterday does not depend on how it felt — but what you noticed matters too, and writing it down makes it yours forever.*
