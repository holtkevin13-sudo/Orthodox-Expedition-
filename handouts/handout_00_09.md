## ✦ Vocabulary — Match Each Word to Its Meaning

Draw a line from each word on the left to its meaning on the right.

  Fasting             •     •  The longest and strictest fast of the
                               Orthodox year — the forty days before
                               Pascha. You will keep your first one
                               next March.

  Wednesday           •     •  Saying *no* to certain foods (meat, dairy,
                               eggs, sometimes fish, sometimes oil) for
                               spiritual reasons — to free the soul, not
                               to harm the body.

  Friday              •     •  The day Christ was crucified. Orthodox
                               Christians fast every week on this day to
                               share, in a small way, in the Cross.

  Great Lent          •     •  The day Judas went to the chief priests
                               to betray Christ. Orthodox Christians
                               fast every week on this day to remember
                               the betrayal — and to repent.

## ✦ Reflection — Write 3 to 5 Sentences

The lesson said fasting is not about the food — it is about the heart. When you kept your first Apostles' Fast, what did you notice? Was it about giving up cheese — or did you notice something deeper happening? Write 3 to 5 sentences. *It's okay if you mostly noticed being hungry. It's okay if you don't know yet. It's okay if you messed up sometimes — the lesson said so. Just write what was real for you.*
