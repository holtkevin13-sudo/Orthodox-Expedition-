## ✦ Vocabulary — Match Each Word to Its Meaning

Draw a line from each word on the left to its meaning on the right.

  Icon                 •     •  The Greek word for *bowing down* — the
                                honor we give to icons, the Cross, and
                                the saints. It is not worship.

  Veneration           •     •  The screen of icons in front of the altar
                                in every Orthodox church. The Royal Doors
                                are at its center.

  Iconostasis          •     •  An image of Christ, the Theotokos, or a
                                saint — written, not just painted —
                                that is a window into the Kingdom of God.

  Theotokos            •     •  Greek for *God-bearer* — the woman who
                                gave birth to God, Mary the Mother of
                                Christ.

## ✦ Reflection — Write 3 to 5 Sentences

The lesson said an icon is a **window**, not a picture. A picture is something you look *at*. An icon is something you look *through*. The saint on the other side is looking back at you. This week you stood in front of one icon at SJTD for a full minute. Write 3 to 5 sentences: which saint did you choose? What did it feel like to be looked at by an icon? Did anything happen that surprised you? *It's okay if nothing dramatic happened. The icon was still looking back.*
