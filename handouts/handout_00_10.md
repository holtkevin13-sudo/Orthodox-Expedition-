## ✦ Vocabulary — Match Each Word to Its Meaning

Draw a line from each word on the left to its meaning on the right.

  Apostle             •     •  *Petros* in Greek, *Cephas* in Aramaic.
                               The new name Christ gave Simon the
                               fisherman — *the one on whom the Church
                               is built.*

  Pillar              •     •  The road on which the risen Christ
                               appeared to Saul, knocking him to the
                               ground and changing him forever.

  Rock                •     •  Greek for *one who is sent.* The twelve
                               men Christ chose, sent out into all the
                               world to preach the Gospel and build
                               the Church.

  Damascus Road       •     •  A title for Peter and Paul together —
                               the *two pillars* of the Orthodox Church.
                               Their feast is June 29.

## ✦ Reflection — Write 3 to 5 Sentences

Peter denied Christ three times. Paul personally hunted down Christians and approved their killings. Both became the greatest Apostles of the Church. The lesson said: *failure does not disqualify.* Write 3 to 5 sentences. Have you ever felt that something you did wrong should disqualify you from being loved? What do Peter and Paul say back to that fear? *It's okay to write that you have felt that. It's okay to keep your answer short. The two pillars are listening.*
