## ✦ Vocabulary — Match Each Word to Its Meaning

Draw a line from each word on the left to its meaning on the right.

  Dormition         •     •  The almond-shape of golden or blue light
                             around Christ in many Orthodox icons. The
                             icon's way of showing divine glory.

  Bier              •     •  Greek for *falling asleep* — the Orthodox
                             name for the Theotokos's death. She fell
                             asleep, and her Son came down to carry
                             her home.

  Mandorla          •     •  The first to enter what is promised to
                             everyone united to Christ — bodies raised,
                             glorified, taken to be with Him forever.
                             The Theotokos has *gone ahead of us* into it.

  First-fruits      •     •  A small low table or bed on which a body
                             is laid for burial. The Dormition icon
                             shows the Theotokos lying on one,
                             surrounded by the weeping Apostles.

## ✦ Layout — Label the Dormition Icon

*Look at the Dormition icon — the one displayed at SJTD this week, or printed for you alongside this handout, or shown to you on a screen.* Find the five parts below. Draw a small arrow from each label to where it appears in the icon.

  ☐  **The bier** — the low table where the Theotokos lies as if asleep

  ☐  **The Theotokos** — eyes closed, hands folded, at peace

  ☐  **The twelve Apostles** — gathered around her, weeping, leaning on each other

  ☐  **Christ** — standing behind the bier, surrounded by the **mandorla** (almond-shape of light)

  ☐  **The swaddled infant** — held in Christ's arms. *That infant is her soul.* In life, she held Him as a baby; in death, He holds her.

## ✦ Reflection — Write 5 to 7 Sentences (this one is longer — it is the close of Topic 00)

Fifteen weeks ago, you opened *The Church That Stayed.* Look back at what you have walked through: the trunk-and-branch tree, the Sign of the Cross, icons, the Theotokos, your Chrismation, your first Communion, the Divine Liturgy, the Apostles' Fast, Peter and Paul, your prayer corner, your patron St. Herman, the Prophet Elias, the Dormition Fast, and now her great feast. **You have arrived.**

Write 5 to 7 sentences. How are you different now from who you were on May 18? What has changed? What was the hardest part? What was the most beautiful part? What do you carry with you into Year 1? *Take real time with this one. There is no wrong answer. It's okay if your answer is just a list. It's okay if some of it is hard to put into words.*
