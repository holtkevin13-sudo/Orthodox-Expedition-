## ✦ Vocabulary — Match Each Word to Its Meaning

Draw a line from each word on the left to its meaning on the right.

  Sign of the Cross     •     •  The two natures of Christ — fully God and
                                  fully man — shown by tucking these two
                                  fingers down into the palm.

  Trinity               •     •  A small prayer made with the right hand,
                                  tracing the shape of the Cross on your
                                  own body — forehead, chest, right
                                  shoulder, left shoulder.

  Three fingers         •     •  The Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit —
                                  three Persons, one God — confessed by
                                  pressing these three fingertips together.

  Two fingers           •     •  Greek for *bowing down* — the honor we
                                  give the Cross, an icon, or a saint,
                                  which is different from worship.

## ✦ Sequence — Number the Four Touches

## ✦ Reflection — Write 3 to 5 Sentences

The lesson said: *your body prays too.* In the Christianity you grew up with, prayer was mostly something you did with your mind and your voice. Now you are learning that the whole person prays — body and soul together. Write 3 to 5 sentences about what it has felt like this week to pray with your hand. Where did you cross yourself? When? Did anything feel different from praying only with your voice? *It's okay if it felt strange. It's okay to write that.*
