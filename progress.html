About These Streaks

Weekly Streak counts the number of weeks in a row you completed your expedition session.

Prayer Streak counts the number of weeks in a row you prayed faithfully — 5 or more days each week, morning or evening. Pilgrimage days are gently set aside.

Reading Streak counts the number of weeks in a row you opened today's Gospel and answered the question — 5 or more days each week.

Each week, one missed day in any lane is gently absorbed — your streak holds. A small gold dot beside the title means grace was used this week. Glory to God for your faithfulness.
