## ✦ Vocabulary — Match Each Word to Its Meaning

Draw a line from each word on the left to its meaning on the right.

  Theotokos          •     •  A title for Mary that means she remained
                              a virgin her whole life. The Orthodox
                              Church teaches that she was a virgin
                              before, during, and after Jesus' birth.

  Ever-Virgin        •     •  The moment when the angel Gabriel came to
   (Aeiparthenos)               Mary and told her she would bear the
                              Son of God — and she said *let it be*.

  Annunciation       •     •  Greek for *God-bearer* — the woman who
                              gave birth to God. Mary did not just
                              give birth to a man; she gave birth to
                              God Himself, made flesh.

  New Eve            •     •  A title for Mary. Eve said *no* to God
                              and brought death; Mary said *yes* to
                              God and brought life. Her *yes* untied
                              the knot Eve's *no* tied.

## ✦ Reflection — Write 3 to 5 Sentences

The lesson ended with the words *she is your Mother now.* Christ gave her to you on the Cross — *behold thy mother* — and the Orthodox Church has always understood He was speaking to every Christian, including you. Write 3 to 5 sentences. Did this land for you, or did it feel strange? What does it mean to you to have her as your Mother — alongside your own mom? *It's okay if you can't put it all into words yet. It's okay to write that it feels strange.*
