## ✦ Sequence — Number the Steps of the Rite

The rite Father Nick will perform on Friday evening has a specific order. Number these from **1** to **8** in the boxes below.

  ☐  Anointing — Father Nick anoints you with Holy Chrism in eight places, sealing each with the words *The Seal of the Gift of the Holy Spirit. Amen.*

  ☐  Renounce the devil — turn west, three times: *I do renounce him.*

  ☐  Tonsure — a tiny clip of your hair in four places, in the shape of a Cross.

  ☐  Confess Christ — turn east, three times: *I do unite myself to Christ.* Then recite the Nicene Creed.

  ☐  First Communion — receive the Body and Blood of Christ for the first time (Friday evening or Sunday morning).

  ☐  Procession — walk around the table three times with Father Nick, your candle in hand, the choir singing *As many as have been baptized into Christ, have put on Christ.*

  ☐  Baptism — three pourings of water at the font: *In the name of the Father. And of the Son. And of the Holy Spirit. Amen.*

  ☐  Blessing of the water — Father Nick prays over the font, calling down the Holy Spirit, then blesses you with the oil of catechumens.
