## ✦ Vocabulary — Match Each Word to Its Meaning

Draw a line from each word on the left to its meaning on the right.

  Orthodox            •     •  One of the twelve men Jesus chose
                                to send out and build His Church.

  Denomination        •     •  Right glory; right belief.
                                From Greek *orthos* (right) +
                                *doxa* (glory or belief).

  Apostle             •     •  A successor of the Apostles, who
                                carries on their authority by the
                                laying-on of hands.

  Bishop              •     •  A branch of Christianity that
                                separated from an older church.

## ✦ Reflection — Write 3 to 5 Sentences

The lesson said: *you are not switching teams — you are coming home.* Think about your life so far — the prayers you've prayed, the Bibles you've read, the people who first taught you about Jesus. Write 3 to 5 sentences about what "coming home" means for **you**, and what you want to thank God for from your past. *It's okay if it's just one or two sentences. It's okay if it's hard to put into words.*
