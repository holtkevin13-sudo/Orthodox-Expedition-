## ✦ Reflection — Write 3 to 5 Sentences

The lesson said: *every fast belongs to someone.* The Apostles' Fast you kept in June belonged to Peter and Paul. The Dormition Fast you just kept belonged to your Mother. Write 3 to 5 sentences. What was different about fasting *for her*? Did the fact that you were fasting *for someone* — your Mother — change how you felt about it? *It's okay if it didn't feel different. It's okay if you mostly noticed being hungry. The fast was still hers, and you walked through it.*
